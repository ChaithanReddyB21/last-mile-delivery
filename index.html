A CLEAR, HONEST MODEL

Built for learning.
Ready to demonstrate.

One warehouse, one vehicle, positive road weights and synthetic customer data. All selected deliveries must complete service before their deadlines. The vehicle returns to the warehouse.

The MST represents infrastructure, not a delivery tour. Knapsack alone gives a capacity-only profit bound; the route solver checks the schedule. Larger plans carry a clear heuristic label.
